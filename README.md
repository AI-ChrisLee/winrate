# Winrate: install in 60 seconds

This skill is a base. Once you have done it your way, tell your squad "update the skill to do it like this."

The Sunday half of an Execution Squad's content lane. It tells you what this week's
content made and which piece it came from, off files your squad already writes.

## Install it

Open Claude Code in your company folder and paste: **"Install this skill:
https://github.com/AI-ChrisLee/winrate. Clone the whole folder into .claude/skills as
winrate, without the .git folder."** Then quit and reopen Claude Code, type `/`, and check
that `winrate` is on the list.

## Run it

On Sunday, say: **"Run winrate."**

It names your newest video past 7 days. Open YouTube Studio, that video, Analytics, last 7
days, and paste the views. That is the only number you fetch yourself. No video that old
yet? It does not stop, and the brief prints `held` in that slot.

## What you get

One screen: what shipped, the 7-day views, the asks with their sources, the money with its
sources, the money with no source on its own line, and one move.

Type what you will do next week, in your own words, and say yes. One row lands under
`## Sundays` in `squad/content-log.md`: the date, what shipped, the views, the asks, the
money, your line. 4 of those rows fill a 4-week plan.

It says which piece made money. It never says which piece won.

## What it will not do

Send anything. Estimate a number. Guess money onto a video. Rank your videos by views.
Write a second row for the same Sunday. Touch your pipeline.
